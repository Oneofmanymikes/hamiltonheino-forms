Meet Naomi Davison.

Naomi Davison has spent over a decade serving as a local and regional councillor, earning a reputation as a practical leader, strong advocate, and trusted voice for residents across York Region.

First elected to Georgina Council in 2010, Naomi has dedicated her public service to supporting families, managing responsible growth, improving community services, and ensuring local voices are heard. Today, she serves as Regional Councillor for the Town of Georgina and York Region.

Beyond public service, Naomi is a small business owner who has operated the Keswick Kumon Centre for more than 20 years. During the COVID-19 pandemic, she joined Southlake Health to help support frontline staff and continues to work with the hospital today. These experiences have given her firsthand insight into the challenges facing families, workers, and businesses throughout Georgina, Bradford West Gwillimbury, East Gwillimbury, and across York–Simcoe.

Naomi and her husband Stuart, a local elementary school teacher, have called Georgina home since 2000, where they raised their two daughters.

Throughout her time on council, Naomi has delivered real results for Georgina, including 87 affordable housing units, the Multi-Use Recreation Centre (MURC) at no cost to taxpayers, on-demand transit service, expanded rural internet access through YorkNet, and stronger food and shelter support for residents. She has also served on numerous committees and boards, bringing together local organizations, businesses, and community leaders to solve problems and strengthen the region.

As a councillor, small business owner, healthcare worker, wife of a teacher, and mother, Naomi understands the challenges people are facing because she lives them every day.

She is stepping forward to bring her local experience to Queen's Park — working to make life more affordable, strengthen healthcare, create more opportunities for young people, and ensure York–Simcoe families always have someone fighting for them.

Naomi's priorities for York–Simcoe

After 8 years of Doug Ford, families in our community are paying higher property taxes, while the educators and healthcare workers who keep our community running aren't getting the respect they deserve.

1

Responsible property taxes

Holding the line on property taxes and delivering real value for what York–Simcoe families pay.

2

Affordable living for working families

Relief from rising costs at the grocery store, on rent, and in everyday life.

3

Healthcare close to home

Better access to family doctors and care for children, parents, seniors, and loved ones.

4

More mental health supports

Expanding access to mental health care so people can get help when and where they need it.

5

Standing up for educators and nurses

Respecting and backing the teachers, nurses, and frontline workers who keep our schools and hospitals running.

6

Housing people can afford

Real options for renters, newcomers, young people, and families trying to stay rooted here.

7

Cleaning up Lake Simcoe

Protecting and restoring Lake Simcoe for the families, wildlife, and communities that depend on it.

8

Safer, stronger neighbourhoods

Communities where people feel safe walking home, opening their business, taking transit, and sending their kids to school.

9

Opportunities for the next generation

Good schools, good jobs, and a future young people can build right here in Ontario.
